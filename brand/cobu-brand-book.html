01 · The idea

The O lifts its lid

COBU is set in a wide, sturdy typeface. Its O is broad enough to be a Dutch oven, so we gave it a lid. Read the name and you already know what we make.

The pot is the letter

The body is the exact O from the wordmark, so the icon and the name always match.

A domed lid

Its thickness matches the letters’ strokes, and it floats a short gap above the pot as if steam had just lifted it.

Handles on the icon

Used on its own, the pot gets two side handles so it reads as cookware at any size.

02 · Logo set

A wordmark and an icon

Use the wordmark wherever there is room. Use the icon when space is square or small, or when the name is already nearby.

03 · Colour versions

Navy on warm white comes first

Choose the version by the background. On photographs, use whichever of navy or warm white gives more contrast.

04 · Space and size

Give it room

Keep a margin of at least x, the height of the lid and knob, on every side. Below the minimum sizes the gap under the lid starts to close.

05 · Misuse

Keep it as drawn

The logo only works exactly as supplied. Always use the master files; never retype COBU in a font or redraw the pot.

Out in the world

09 · The name

How to write COBU

The name is always written in capitals, in text and in the logo.

Write

COBU

“The new COBU dispenser is on Amazon.”

Avoid

Cobu · cobu · C.O.B.U.

In running text these look like typos or a different brand.
